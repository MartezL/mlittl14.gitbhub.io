JDM Buyer Resources

Use these resources to research vehicles, check import requirements, and find replacement or performance parts. Always verify current regulations before purchasing or shipping a vehicle.

Helpful JDM Resources

Resource Name Resource Type Resource Link How to Use
BE FORWARD Car Marketplace Visit Website Browse Japanese used vehicles, compare prices and specifications, and review shipping options before choosing a car.
Goo Net Car Marketplace Visit Website Research Japanese auction vehicles and exporter services. Auction information can help you compare condition and pricing before buying.
NHTSA Vehicle Importation Guidelines Regulation / Law View Guidelines Check U.S. federal vehicle-safety import requirements. This is especially important for confirming whether a vehicle qualifies under the 25-year rule.
U.S. EPA Vehicle Import Information Regulation / Law Visit Website Review emissions-related import requirements and exemptions before arranging shipment of a JDM vehicle to the United States.

Resource Type Definitions

Car Marketplace
A website where buyers can search vehicles offered by dealers, exporters, or auctions. Use marketplaces to compare models, prices, mileage, condition, and availability.
Regulation / Law
An official government source explaining the legal requirements for importing and registering a vehicle. Check these sources before paying for a car because eligibility can depend on age, safety standards, emissions rules, and destination.
Parts Marketplace
A website that sells OEM, replacement, used, or performance parts. Checking parts availability can help you estimate the long-term cost and difficulty of owning a specific JDM vehicle.

Before You Buy

Use more than one resource when researching a vehicle. A marketplace can help you find the car, government sources can help confirm whether it can legally be imported, and parts marketplaces can help you estimate what ownership may cost after the purchase.
